#📍 Region📶 Frequency range📈 |Vo / Ii|🔄 Phase of −Vo / Ii📊 Width on this plot (decades)

The shaded band is the 0.1× to 10× range of the slider. The plot runs wider, 0.01× to 100×, so you can see where the plateau ends. Dashed gray lines are the two asymptotes: 1/(ω·Cc) below the zero and 1/gmc above it. The phase rises after the zero, which is what makes it a left half plane zero. A right half plane zero gives the same flattening in magnitude but the phase keeps falling.

5. Model and values

Linear small-signal phasor model, not a transistor-level simulation. Fixed values: Ii = 1 µA, R1 = ro2 = 100 kΩ. The vb device is modeled as an ideal common gate: 1/gmc looking into its source, and all of its source current delivered to the V1 node. The zero is at gmc/Cc and the pole pair is near √(gm2·gmc / (C1·CL)), so the plateau widens with larger Cc and gm2 and with smaller CL, C1 and gmc. The pole pair is lightly damped in this ideal model, which is the peak at the end of the plateau. Both value sets are illustrative, so replace them with your own.
